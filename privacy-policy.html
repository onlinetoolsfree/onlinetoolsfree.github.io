Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 4, 2025

At Online Tools Free, accessible from https://onlinetoolsfree.github.io/, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights related to it.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information such as names, email addresses, or IP addresses. Our tools are designed to work directly in your browser without storing or transmitting data to a server.

2. Cookies

We do not use cookies for tracking or advertising. However, third-party services such as analytics or embedded content may use cookies for their own functionality.

3. Analytics

We may use privacy-focused analytics services to understand basic usage patterns. These services do not collect personally identifiable information.

4. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices or content.

5. Children’s Privacy

Our website is safe for all ages and does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.

6. Data Security

Since we do not store user data, there is minimal risk of a data breach. However, we take basic precautions to ensure the site remains secure and safe to use.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised “Last Updated” date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at onlinetoolsfree.contact@gmail.com.
