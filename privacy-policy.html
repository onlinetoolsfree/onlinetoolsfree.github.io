Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 1, 2025

At Online Tools Free, available at https://onlinetoolsfree.github.io/, your privacy is our priority. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our tools and services. By using our website, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We respect your privacy and do not collect any personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or IP address. Most of our tools are built to function entirely within your browser. Your data is processed locally and is not stored, logged, or transmitted to any server owned or operated by us.

2. Non-Personal Data Collection

We may use third-party analytics tools like Google Analytics, Simple Analytics, or similar platforms to understand general usage patterns. These tools collect anonymous, aggregated data such as device type, browser type, pages visited, and time spent. This information helps us improve the user experience but does not identify you personally.

3. Cookies

We do not use cookies for tracking or profiling users. However, third-party services embedded in our website (such as ads, analytics, or external scripts) may use cookies independently. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party services you interact with through our platform.

4. Use of Information

The limited data we analyze (such as anonymous usage stats) is used solely to enhance performance, detect errors, add new features, and ensure security. We do not rent, sell, or share any user data with third parties.

5. Data Security

Our site uses GitHub Pages, a secure hosting platform maintained by GitHub. We take reasonable precautions to ensure that our code is safe, tools are sandboxed, and third-party services are trusted. However, because we do not store personal data, the risk of breach is extremely low.

6. External Links

Our website may link to other external websites or tools. We do not control or endorse the privacy policies or practices of any third-party websites. Please review their policies before using those services.

7. Children’s Privacy

This website is safe for all age groups and does not knowingly collect any data from children under 13. If we learn that a child has submitted personal information, we will delete it promptly.
